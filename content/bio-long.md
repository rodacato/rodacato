Adrian Castillo is a backend and platform engineer based in Colima, Mexico, with over 17 years of experience shipping production systems — from fintech payment platforms and HIPAA-compliant healthcare APIs to cryptocurrency exchanges and multi-provider LLM gateways. His core stack is Ruby and Rails, with deep experience in Node.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, and growing investment in Rust and Python. He designs systems around Domain-Driven Design, hexagonal architecture, and event-driven patterns, with a discipline for high test coverage and explicit contracts over clever abstractions.

On the AI side, Adrian builds infrastructure rather than just consuming tools — projects like SheLLM (a unified REST API for LLM providers) and KenoBot (an AI assistant with 4-tier cognitive memory architecture) reflect his focus on API ergonomics, cost visibility, and operational reliability. He writes at notdefined.dev and believes the best developer experience is the one you don't notice. Rails Rumble 2015 winner. Prefers boring infrastructure that works over exciting infrastructure that doesn't.
